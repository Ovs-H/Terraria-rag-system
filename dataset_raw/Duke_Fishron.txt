This article is about the Hardmode boss. For the quest fish, see 
Fishron
. For the aquatic mount, see 
Cute Fishron

Pigron

Duke Fishron
 is a 
Hardmode
 
boss
. While he can be summoned as soon as Hardmode is initiated, his difficulty is on par with that of a post-Golem boss, making him a serious threat to unprepared players. Fighting Duke Fishron is not required for 
game progression
, but his defeat rewards the player with powerful weapons and equipment.
While Duke Fishron is alive, his exclusive music 
Duke Fishron
 will play. When 
Otherworldly music
 is enabled, the track 
Boss 2 (Otherworldly)
 will play instead.
Summoning
[
edit
]
Duke Fishron is summoned by 
fishing
 in the 
Ocean
 at any time, using a 
Truffle Worm
 as 
bait
 
(see 
Notes
 for details)
. Once the bobber bobs, reeling in the line spawns Duke Fishron, who emerges from the water, pauses for a moment, and then begins attacking the player. Duke Fishron is invulnerable during his summoning animation.
Duke Fishron himself does not require any bosses to be defeated prior, meaning he can be summoned earlier than his intended stage of progression, and that Truffle Worms brought to a new world can be used to summon him.
Behavior
[
edit
]
First phase
[
edit
]
Duke Fishron lunges at the player exactly five times, traveling through blocks, before using one of two projectile attacks in the following order:
Duke Fishron blows 21 
Detonating Bubbles
[
5
]
 from his mouth that follow the player at moderate speeds before exploding and dealing damage while inflicting the 
Wet
 
debuff
 for 3–8 seconds. The Detonating Bubble has the following features:
Can also be destroyed in a single hit from any attack or projectile.
Movement is affected by 
wind
.
Hitbox will suddenly increase in size when it explodes (i.e. dealing damage to players further away).
Duke Fishron will either move to the top left or top right of the player's location before starting this attack. The Sharkron attack has the opposite direction of this attack changes each time Duke Fishron is summoned.
Duke Fishron waves his fins, summoning two Sharknado Bolts that move in bottom-left and bottom-right directions (bypassing any 
platforms
) until they travel 1.5× the vertical size of the screen or land on solid blocks or liquids. The bolts then disperse and spawn Sharknados: giant water tornadoes which are 29 tiles high, last 9 seconds
[
6
]
, and each fire six 
Sharkrons
 directly at the player. Sharkrons have limited flight range, are destroyed upon colliding with blocks, and can be killed directly by the player.
Second phase
[
edit
]
When Duke Fishron's health falls below 50% (30,000 / 
39,000
 / 
49,725
), he remains stationary for a second and transforms (and remains invulnerable to 
damage
 while transforming). Upon transitioning into his second phase, Duke Fishron's skin becomes a darker green and his eyes will begin glowing a bright yellow. Duke Fishron's damage and speed increase significantly, but his defense is reduced to 40. His lunges also become faster and are only performed three times instead of five before executing one of his projectile attacks, which are also upgraded:
Detonating Bubbles are now fired while Duke Fishron flies in quick loops, leaving a trail of bubbles behind him. This attack fires a total of 31 Detonating Bubbles
[
7
]
, and will start in the same location relative to the player as the first phase's Detonating Bubble attack.
Duke Fishron now only fires one Sharknado Bolt, but it will quickly home in on the player and create a Cthulunado [
sic
] on the ground directly underneath them (if the player is 95 tiles (190 feet) above the surface directly below them, the bolt will instead detonate on top of them). Cthulunados are significantly larger than Sharknados (58 tiles high, 23 tiles wide, 11 tiles from the center), last for 14 seconds
[
6
]
, and will spawn twice as many Sharkrons while dealing double the contact damage of regular Sharknados. Additionally, the Sharkrons fired from Cthulunados deal higher amounts of damage to the player than they do ordinarily from Sharknados.
Third phase
[
edit
]
Expert and Master Mode-Only Content
: This information applies 
only
 to 
Expert Mode
, 
Master Mode
, and worlds with these modes.
When Duke Fishron's health is reduced to below 15% (
11,700
 / 
14,917
), he will stop moving, then transform again as the background becomes covered in a dark, blue fog. Duke Fishron turns invisible, but his eyes still glow. His contact damage is reduced to 
184
 / 
277
, his defense will drop all the way to 0, and he will only attack by charging at the player, but gains the ability to teleport near them before doing so. Duke Fishron momentarily becomes visible as he is charging at the player, and his charges will be performed in a set pattern:
First, he will charge once before teleporting to one side of the player.
Then, he will charge twice, and teleport to the other side of the player.
Finally, he will charge three times, teleport to one side of the player, and the pattern will reset.
During this phase, the side he teleports to will always be the opposite side he last dashed towards.
After being defeated, the background will return to normal.
Enraged state
[
edit
]
Straying too far from the surface 
Ocean
 area during the fight causes Duke Fishron to "enrage", causing him to lunge at the player continuously, deal double damage, gain double defense, as well as becoming faster and firing more frequent and faster Cthulunados.
The "surface Ocean area" is limited in both horizontal and vertical directions. The player is only considered to be in this area if they are within the following bounds:
[
1
]
Horizontal: within 400 tiles from the (unreachable) true world edge (about 360 tiles from the visible world edge).
Vertical: more than 50 tiles away from the top of the world (about 10 tiles from the visible top of the world), and above the 
underground
 layer.
Debuffs
[
edit
]
Upon hitting the player in 
Expert Mode
 or 
Master Mode
, Duke Fishron has a 
1/3 (33.33%)
 chance to inflict 
Bleeding
 and a 
1/3 (33.33%)
 chance to inflict 
Feral Bite
. He can only inflict one of the two debuffs at a time, never both at once.
Despawning
[
edit
]
Duke Fishron will despawn if all players are dead or if the targeted player is more than 350 tiles away from him.

Notes
[
edit
]
Duke Fishron can be summoned by fishing in 
honey
 or 
lava
 in addition to 
water
.
The following conditions regarding outer circumstances need to be met for being able to summon Duke Fishron:
[
8
]
The bobber is within 380 tiles from the (unreachable) true world edge (about 340 tiles from the visible world edge).
This means that Duke Fishron can be summoned at any height.
The bobber is in a pool of 
water
 or 
lava
 which is larger than 1000 tiles (following the 
lake size rules
) or in a pool of 
honey
 which is larger than 668 tiles.
The usual 
requirements for fishing in lava
 (e.g., a 
Hotline Fishing Hook
) do not apply when summoning Duke Fishron. He can be summoned from lava with any fishing pole and without special accessories.
Duke Fishron is currently not alive anywhere in the world.
While fishing with a 
Truffle Worm
 in the 
Ocean
 while the 
Sonar Potion
's effect is active, no text is shown for indicating Duke Fishron is about to be summoned.
In 
Zenith
 and 
Remix
 worlds, Duke Fishron will drop the 
Aqua Scepter
 instead of the Bubble Gun.

Tips
[
edit
]
For more elaborate strategies on defeating Duke Fishron, including 
weapon
 and 
arena
 recommendations, see 
Guide:Duke Fishron strategies
.
The player should keep in mind their distance from the 
Ocean
 to prevent Duke Fishron from becoming enraged. One option is to build solid blocks around the Ocean boundaries to prevent accidentally enraging him.
The 
Inferno Potion
 can come in handy during this fight to prevent taking any damage from Duke Fishron's Detonating Bubbles. While there is a slight delay between the damage intervals of the Inferno Potion's ring of fire, it is capable of clearing out groups of bubbles all at once.
Since Detonating Bubbles have a larger damaging area when they explode, the player should still keep some distance from them even when using the Inferno Potion.
Players can build a platform along the top of the Ocean to avoid sinking. 
Water Walking Boots
, their upgrades, or 
Water Walking Potions
 can be used as a substitute for platforms.
Duke Fishron's charges aim for where the player is, not where they will be. Because of this, moving perpendicularly with his charges will cause him to completely miss. Dodging his charges can be as simple as moving in a zig-zag pattern or a triangle, or utilizing 
dashing
 equipment such as the 
Crystal Assassin armor
, 
Shield of Cthulhu
, 
Tabi
, or 
Master Ninja Gear
. The high mobility of the 
Winged Slime Mount
 can help as well.
Should Duke Fishron and his Cthulunados be hard to see, the player can use 
Shine Potion
 and 
Hunter Potion
. The 
Ichor
 debuff can also be inflicted on the boss to highlight him.
Using an 
Anti-Gravity Hook
 on a block and continuously going in circles can successfully allow dodging every attack and dash from the boss when it is enraged.
In 
Expert Mode
, the Shield of Cthulhu can be used to great effect, providing brief 
invincibility frames
 when dashing into him.
The 
Chillet
 mount can be used to a similar effect since it too provides brief invulnerability.
Due to Duke Fishron's fast speed in its third phase, it is suggested to use 
platforms
 in the arena to assist the player's movement.
Though his difficulty makes him a post-
Plantera
 or 
Golem
 boss, it is possible to defeat him before the 
mechanical bosses
. Obtaining his weapons this early will make a significant portion of Hardmode (including the fights against the mechanical bosses) much easier to progress through.
If fighting Duke Fishron after at least one 
mechanical boss
 has been defeated, and 500 max health has been obtained from 
Life Fruits
, crafting 
Jungle Juice
 can give a slight edge, as it heals 30 more health than a 
Greater Healing Potion
.
Trivia
[
edit
]
 
Early concept art of Duke Fishron by 
Lazure
.
[
9
]
[
10
]
Duke Fishron was originally part of an 
April Fools
' joke by 
Re-Logic
 that claimed an "Aquarian Horde" 
event
 was to be included in the game.
[
9
]
 The Duke Fishron element alone ended up being introduced.
Duke Fishron (and by extension, the 
Cute Fishron
) appears to be a combination of a shark, a pig, and a dragon. 
Cenx
 confirmed that the 
Pigron
 was a cross between the latter two creatures, which further proves this theory.
Duke Fishron turns transparent when over blocks, similar to Pigrons.
Duke Fishron's Sharknado attack is an allusion to the 2013 American made-for-television disaster film 
Sharknado
.
The Cthulunado could be a reference to Cthulhu's avatar the "Father of All Sharks".
[
11
]
The sprite of Duke Fishron's 
Treasure Bag
 more closely resembles a pig, as it lacks the fangs that Duke Fishron has.
Duke Fishron's weapon drops have considerably low 
reforge
 costs compared to other weapons in the same stage of progression.
Duke Fishron is one of three bosses with a corresponding 
minion
 (
Tempest Staff
), the others being the 
Martian Saucer
 (
Xeno Staff
) and 
The Twins
 (
Optic Staff
).
Duke Fishron is the only boss with three distinct phases, one being 
Expert Mode
-exclusive.
Duke Fishron has an unused attack where he flies in quick loops similar to one of his phase 2 attacks, but does not spawn any Detonating Bubbles.
[
12
]
Judging by where it is placed within Duke Fishron's AI code, it can be assumed this is an unfinished attack that was intended for the boss' third phase.
Along with the Empress of Light and 
Queen Slime
, Duke Fishron is one of the only 
Hardmode
 bosses that does not have to be defeated to progress the game.
When killed, the 
gore
 shows that Duke Fishron has blue blood, similar to the 
Empress of Light
. 
Blue blood
 is an idiom for noble birth or descent, befitting the boss's title of 
Duke
.
Duke Fishron shares several similarities with the Empress of Light.
Both are optional bosses intended to be fought post-
Golem
, but may be summoned early.
Their summoning item is a 
critter
 that cannot be used directly from the inventory to summon the boss.
They both drop a set of 
wings
 themed after themselves.
Their gore sprites have blue blood in reference to the title of nobility in their names.
 The 
Bestiary
 entry for  Duke Fishron: 
"
An aquatic pigron mutation from the depths of the ocean who surfaces in search of a rare, savory type of worm.
"
This suggests that Duke Fishron is a mutated Pigron.
