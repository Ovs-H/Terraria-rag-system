The Destroyer
 is a 
Hardmode
 
mechanical boss
 that is essentially a more difficult version of the 
Eater of Worlds
. Its segments share a single health pool, meaning individual segments cannot die. Each of The Destroyer's segments fires lasers at the player, and as they take damage, they launch 
Probes
, independent 
flying enemies
 that fire lasers and can be killed individually.
While The Destroyer is alive, its exclusive music 
The Destroyer
 will play. When 
Otherworldly music
 is enabled, the track 
Boss 2 (Otherworldly)
 will play instead.
Summoning and spawning
[
edit
]
Manual
[
edit
]
The Destroyer can be summoned manually using a 
Mechanical Worm
 at 
night
 (from 7:30 PM to 4:30 AM).
Random
[
edit
]
The Destroyer can also spawn naturally at the start of the night, as long as it has not yet been defeated at least once in the current world. Its arrival when spawning naturally is announced by the 
status message
 
"
You feel vibrations from deep below...
"
.
In order for the boss to spawn naturally, the following conditions must be fulfilled at dusk (7:30 PM):
[
2
]
The world is in 
Hardmode
 and at least one 
Demon or Crimson Altar
 has been destroyed.
In worlds where the 
Skyblock
 
special world seed
 is active and the 
tile threshold
 is not exceeded, The Destroyer can spawn even if no altars have been destroyed.
No boss is already alive, and the 
Eye of Cthulhu
 is not already spawning naturally.
None of the following 
events
 are active: 
Lantern Night
, 
Old One's Army
, 
Pumpkin Moon
, or 
Frost Moon
.
Time is not being accelerated by the 
Enchanted Sundial
 or 
Enchanted Moondial
.
If the conditions are met, there is a 
1/10 (10%)
 chance for a random mechanical boss to spawn that has not yet been defeated in the world. This gives The Destroyer initially a spawn chance of 
1/30 (3.33%)
 per night. If another mechanical boss is defeated first, the chance for The Destroyer to spawn increases to 
1/20 (5%)
, and should both of the other mechanical bosses be defeated first, then The Destroyer has the full remaining 
1/10 (10%)
 spawn chance per night.
Once The Destroyer has been selected to spawn, the status message appears and a hidden countdown of 1 minute 21 seconds begins. The Destroyer spawns at the end of the countdown, at 8:51 PM. Another check is performed at that time, with all of the following conditions needing to be fulfilled in order for the boss to spawn:
[
3
]
At least one player is in the 
surface
 or 
Space
 layer.
Consequently, the natural spawning of The Destroyer can easily be prevented by being in a layer lower than the surface at 8:51 PM. Returning to the surface afterward will not cause The Destroyer to spawn either (unlike with the Eye of Cthulhu).
This restriction is lifted in worlds where the 
world spawn point
 is below the surface, such as worlds with the 
special world seed
 
Remix
 active or the 
secret world seed
 
Mole people
. The Destroyer can spawn naturally in those worlds regardless of the player's position, whether they are above or below the surface.
No boss is already alive.
The 
Lunar Events
 are not active and the countdown for the spawning of the 
Moon Lord
 has not begun, either due to manual summoning or defeating the fourth 
Celestial Pillar
.
Time is not being accelerated by the Enchanted Sundial or Enchanted Moondial.
In singleplayer, exiting the world during the countdown also prevents The Destroyer from spawning naturally.
In worlds where the 
special seeds
 
For the Worthy
 and 
The Constant
 are present but 
Celebration Mk 10
 is not, The Destroyer will still be able to randomly spawn, even if it has been defeated before.
For the Worthy and Remix
[
edit
]
The Destroyer cannot be summoned in worlds where both the 
For the Worthy
 and Remix special world seeds are active, since the boss is part of 
Mechdusa
 instead.

Behavior
[
edit
]
The Destroyer spawns coiled up, like most worm enemies, and immediately starts uncoiling and following the player. It has limited mobility when fully in the air, requiring 
blocks
 or 
platforms
 to "burrow" through. However, it is able to move freely while off-screen. It follows the player from any distance and will only despawn if the player dies or if dawn comes (4:30 AM). It despawns by quickly traveling underground until off-screen.
The Destroyer performs the following attacks:
It leaps from the ground and attempts to ram the player.
It attempts to surround the player with its segments.
It fires red Death Lasers from its segments which deal 44 / 
72
 / 
108
 damage.
[
4
]
It releases flying 
Probes
 from its body that fire Pink Lasers which deal 50 / 
88
 / 
132
 damage.
[
5
]
Each body segment has a 
1/25 (4%)
 chance to release a Probe whenever it is damaged. This only happens once per segment and is signified by the red light on the segment turning off.
[
6
]
 Probes will fly towards the player, attempting to maintain a distance while occasionally moving into melee range. They fire lasers with moderate accuracy.
Probes never return to the main body, and will not despawn as long as The Destroyer is alive, even if the player goes too far away.
[
7
]
Killing the boss itself does not destroy the remaining Probes, and they will still continue to attack the player afterwards.
Since each body segment can only spawn one Probe, The Destroyer will eventually run out of Probes to spawn.
[
4
]
Dim segments still fire lasers like normal.
[
4
]

Tips
[
edit
]
For more elaborate strategies on defeating The Destroyer, including 
weapon
 and 
arena
 recommendations, see 
Guide:The Destroyer strategies
.
Since The Destroyer follows the player instead of despawning, one can use any means of teleportation (
Magic Mirrors
, 
Teleporters
, etc.) to easily avoid it during the fight.
Keep in mind that The Destroyer can move freely without blocks while it is off-screen.
As with the Eater of Worlds, weapons that can hit several enemies are highly effective against The Destroyer.
For extra risk and reward, line up with the head (which has no defense) and fire down the length of its body.
As The Destroyer is immune to 
debuffs
, the player should avoid using 
flasks
 and weapons heavily dependent on debuffs.
Using a 
Gravitation Potion
 can help avoid contact with The Destroyer by hovering just above it.
Probes
 should be taken out as soon as possible, since they interfere with the fight and additionally serve as a steady source of 
Hearts
.
Using the 
minimap
 to see where The Destroyer will surface can greatly help with dodging.
Trivia
[
edit
]
With 
280
 / 
420
 damage, The Destroyer's head has the third-highest damage of any boss in 
Expert
 and 
Master Mode
. It is second only to the 
Moon Lord
's Phantasmal Deathray and the enraged 
Empress of Light
.
The Destroyer is extremely long, measuring 82 segments including head and tail, or 239 tiles in length.
[
9
]
Because the 
Compass
 and 
Depth Meter
 reveal each tile to be 2 feet in length, The Destroyer would be about 159.33 yards (174.25 m) long.
In 
For the Worthy
 worlds, The Destroyer has 100 body segments (102 in total), and each segment is bigger.
The Destroyer has the highest total health of any 
boss
 except the Moon Lord. It still has the largest single health bar, as the Moon Lord's total health is the combination of all its body parts.
The Destroyer has many small metal devices jutting out of it that resemble 
centipede
-like legs or wheels.
Some teasers for the 
 Mobile version
 release included The Destroyer having blue-lit segments instead of red.
Verify
It is theoretically possible to defeat The Destroyer without having it spawn any Probes.
Verify
 – According to the 
Terraria
 lore pages
, The Destroyer was one of the three mechanical recreations of Cthulhu's missing organs. In the 
Bestiary
, it is revealed to be a simulacrum of Cthulhu's spine. Despite being heavily influenced from in design, the 
Eater of Worlds
 has no connection to Cthulhu and is not mentioned to be a former body part in its Bestiary entry.
[
10
]
 The 
Bestiary
 entry for  The Destroyer: 
"
A mechanical simulacrum of Cthulhu's spine decorated in laser-armed probes, which detach from its body when damaged.
"
