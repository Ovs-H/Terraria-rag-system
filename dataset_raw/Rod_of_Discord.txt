The 
Rod of Discord
 is a 
Hardmode
 
tool
 that has a 
1/500 (0.2%)
 / 
1/400 (0.25%)
 chance of being dropped by 
Chaos Elementals
. When used, it instantly teleports the player to the location of the cursor and inflicts the 
Chaos State
 
debuff
 on the player for 6 seconds. Using the Rod of Discord while this debuff is active causes the player to lose health equal to 
1/7 (14.29%)
 of their maximum 
health
.
It can be upgraded into the 
Rod of Harmony
 by throwing it into 
Shimmer
 after the 
Moon Lord
 has been defeated.

Notes
[
edit
]
In worlds with the 
Celebration Mk 10
 seed, the drop rate of the Rod of Discord is increased to 
1/100 (1%)
 regardless of 
world difficulty
.
The Rod of Discord cannot be used to teleport into the 
Jungle Temple
 prior to defeating 
Plantera
.
More precisely,
[
1
]
 the player will not be teleported pre-Plantera to any location that is below the 
surface
 and has an 
unsafe
 
Lihzahrd Brick Wall
 as 
background wall
. In worlds with the 
Remix
 seed enabled, this restriction extends above the surface.
The health loss inflicted by Chaos State cannot be reduced in any way or negated through 
dodge
 effects. It also bypasses and does not trigger 
invincibility frames
. It does not bypass invincibility from 
Godmode
, however.
The Rod of Discord's teleport range is limited to 60 tiles horizontally and 37.5 tiles vertically from the player's center
[
2
]
.
The target location must be at least 3 tiles high and 2 tiles wide. Pressing the 
⚒
 Use / Attack
 button will always place the player's feet at the cursor position. Thus, in order to teleport into a 3-tile-high opening, the player must aim at the floor. Unsuccessful teleports play the same sound, but do not inflict the debuff.
While riding a 
mount
, the target location must usually be higher, depending on the mount's height. For instance, while riding the 
Unicorn Mount
, the location must be 5 tiles high.
Teleporting resets 
fall damage
 to begin at the height at which the player teleported to.
Using the Rod of Discord when moving maintains the player's momentum.
Using it does not cancel 
Shroomite armor
's stealth.
If the player only taps the 
⚒
 Use / Attack
 button with the Rod of Discord on the 
 Mobile version
, they will be teleported slightly to the right (and above if blocks or furniture are obstructing the path). Using the thumbpad can yield mixed results if the aim mode is set to "Aim & Use" rather than "Aim Only".
The Rod of Discord cannot be used to teleport past 
Unbreakable Walls
 (generated naturally in 
Dual Dungeons
 worlds).
[
3
]
Tips
[
edit
]
The Rod of Discord is arguably the most powerful movement tool in the game; besides being an obvious boon to traversing terrain, it allows the player to dodge high-damage or difficult to avoid attacks, including the 
Moon Lord
's Phantasmal Deathray, contact damage from the 
Dungeon Guardian
, and several of the 
Empress of Light
's attacks. It is also very useful in helping to catch 
Truffle Worms
.
While it is one of the rarest items in the game, it can be obtained immediately after the start of 
Hardmode
.
Since this item is one of the few ways to deal self-damage on command, it can be used with the 
Shrimpy Truffle
 or the 
Panic Necklace
 to activate the speed boost. Furthermore, it allows easy obtainment of 
The Frequent Flyer
 
achievement
.
Trivia
[
edit
]
Dying from teleporting during the Chaos State will display unique 
death messages
:
"
<Player name> didn't materialize
"
"
<Player name>'s legs appeared where their head should be.
"
The abbreviation of the Rod of Discord, 
RoD
, is the same as the first word, 
rod
.
In earlier mobile versions, using the Rod of Discord would instantly kill its user, and the death message would read "Structures".
Despite not being a weapon, the Rod of Discord can be placed on a 
Weapon Rack
.
The Rod of Discord emits pink light when dropped. It is also fully bright in the dark when used or placed inside a 
Weapon Rack
, an 
Item Frame
, or an 
Item Flask
.
Successfully using the Rod of Discord leaves behind pink glowing particles at the location of the player, as well as produces those particles at the teleport location. It also coats the player with glowing particles which gradually lose their glow as they disappear.
