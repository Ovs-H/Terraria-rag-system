The 
Terra Blade
 is a 
Hardmode
, post-
Plantera
 
broadsword
. When swung, a green aura follows the swing, reaching farther than the sword itself. The aura can hit up to three 
enemies
. The sword will also fire a green slash projectile that pierces infinitely, dealing the weapon's damage but reducing its damage by 25% of its current damage for each target it pierces.

 upon contact with 
blocks
, or when its damage falls to 0 after piercing.

modifier
 is 
Legendary

Notes
[
edit
]
The Terra Blade only receives 75% of any 
melee speed
 bonus.
The Terra Blade will always 
knock enemies away
 from the player, regardless of the direction it is swung.
Size bonuses from the 
Titan Glove
 (and its upgrades) and from 
modifiers
 increase not only the weapon's swing reach, but also the size of the slash projectile.
[
4
]
The Terra Blade is fully bright in the dark. Its aura, projectile, and slashes that they create are also fully bright in the dark. The aura, projectile, and slashes also leave behind light-emitting particles which are also fully bright in the dark.

Tips
[
edit
]
Due to the fast speed and high damage of the projectiles, it is very effective to use the Terra Blade even at long distances. However, using the close ranged swing is important to maximize the damage output.
Because the Terra Blade shoots a projectile that can penetrate through enemies, it is highly effective when fighting against hordes of enemies or for events such as the 
Old One's Army
 and 
Martian Madness
.
When seeking to craft the Terra Blade, the player should gradually get all the materials needed as they progress through the game.
Unlike other projectile-firing melee weapons, the Terra Blade's projectile fire rate scales with the main sword's attack speed. Due to this, it gets a large boost from melee speed buffs.
Trivia
[
edit
]
Crafting the Terra Blade requires obtaining a total of eight different usable swords.
The Terra Blade's functionality is a combination of both of its ingredient weapons: the piercing projectiles of the 
True Night's Edge
 and the extended melee slash attacks of the 
True Excalibur
.
The Terra Blade resembles the 
Night's Edge
, featuring a curved blade with holes in its side.
The Terra Blade appears in two video games as part of a crossover: 
Dungeon Defenders 2
 and 
Palworld
.
