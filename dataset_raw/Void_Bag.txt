The Void Bag spawning the portal. Notice the purple light emitted from the portal.
The 
Void Bag
 is a 
portable storage
 item that grants the player access to the 
Void Vault
 anywhere. When used, it will summon a temporary portal which lasts for 3 minutes, and pressing 
⚷
 Open / Activate
 on the portal opens the player's Void Vault inventory. The Void Bag is never consumed and can be used limitlessly.
Pressing 
⚷
 Open / Activate
 on the Void Bag item in the inventory turns it into the 
Closed Void Bag
. Pressing the button again opens the Void Bag again.
When opened, the Void Vault storage will effectively act as the player's secondary inventory, which automatically stores items collected after the player's main inventory is full, indicated by a purple mask on the pickup text. Additionally, it also allows the player to interact with the items inside it as if they were in the main inventory without opening the Void Vault storage interface.
When closed, the Void Bag functions similarly to the 
Money Trough
 / 
Eye Bone
, only acting as a regular 
portable storage
 item. This will prevent it from picking up items when the player has a full inventory, and it loses the function of the secondary inventory.

Notes
[
edit
]
The color for the mask on the pickup text is 
 
#320866
.
[
1
]
An opened Void Bag will act effectively as the player's secondary inventory, which inherits the following functions:
Consuming 
food
 and 
potions
 in it with 
Quick Heal
, 
Quick Mana
, and 
Quick Buff
.
Crafting with its contents.
Quick Stack to Nearby Chests
.
When looting all items from a 
storage item
 with a full main inventory, the items will be sent to the Void Vault.
Favoriting items (to prevent quick stacking).
Items that have a function in the player's inventory will work as normal, including the 
Lucky Clover
, 
informational accessories
, 
Guide to Critter Companionship
, 
Guide to Environmental Preservation
, etc.
However, placing the 
Encumbering Stone
 inside the Void Vault will not prevent the player or an opened Void Bag from picking up items.
Coins
 placed inside of the Void Bag can be used to purchase items, but will not drop upon death.
The Void Bag is only obtainable after defeating 
Skeletron
, as its crafting recipe requires 
Bones
, which drop from enemies in the 
Dungeon
.
However, defeating the 
Eater of Worlds
/
Brain of Cthulhu
 is not necessary: While summoning the boss at least once is required, 
Shadow Scales
 or 
Tissue Samples
 can be obtained by merely destroying a few segments of the Eater of Worlds or a few 
Creepers
, respectively; the boss does not have to be defeated.
In a 
drunk world
, it is obtainable before defeating Skeletron, as Dungeon enemies can spawn prior to defeating the boss.
The Void Bag will not pick up 
quest fish
 if the player's inventory is full or contains another quest fish of the same type. However, the fish can still be placed inside the Void Vault manually, and any quest fish already within the Void Vault will not prevent the player from picking up another in their inventory.
When using items that consume 
ammunition
, the Void Bag will not let the player use any ammo stored within the Void Bag, including ammo used by 
tools
 (e.g. 
Wire
).
Despite coins being able to purchase items when placed inside the Void Bag, the 
Coin Gun
 cannot use the coins inside as ammo.
The Void Bag will not pick up 
Etherian Mana
, but it can be placed into the Void Vault manually or via 
Quick Stack
.
The evil crafting material dropped from decrafting the Void Bag into 
Shimmer
 is dependent on the 
world evil
; it will drop 
Shadow Scales
 in 
Corruption
 worlds and 
Tissue Samples
 in 
Crimson
 worlds.
In 
Drunk
 worlds, the two world evils switch every in-game day, meaning the Void Bag will alternate between both materials each day.
The Void Bag always defaults to the open variant in the 
Journey Mode
 menu when 
researched
, regardless of which variant is used to research it.

The earlier version of the Void Bag's portal.
Earlier versions of the Void Bag during development had a dark purple portal, more resembling the portals from the 
Potion of Return
 in the current game.
The Void Bag's portal is known internally as 
VoidLens
.
