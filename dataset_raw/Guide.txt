The 
Guide
 is usually the first 
NPC
 a player encounters. He spawns near the player when the 
world
 is created. He provides tips mainly aimed at new players via the “
Help
” option and displays 
crafting recipes
 (including the 
crafting station
 required) of items shown to him via the “
Crafting
” option.
Unlike most other NPCs, the Guide spawns upon world creation, before a 
house
 is available. He will, however, move into the first house built. If he is killed, a proper house will need to be built before he can respawn, but he has no additional requirements.
The Guide will defend himself against nearby 
enemies
 using a 
Wooden Bow
, firing 
Wooden Arrows
 initially and then upgrading to 
Flaming Arrows
 during 
Hardmode
. He deals 8 / 
12
 / 
14
 damage at the start of the game, and gains 
various stat boosts
 as the player progresses.

Notes
[
edit
]
(Desktop version)
 When checking condition for name-based NPC drops, the game specifically requires the NPC's name to be using the corresponding localization string.
[
2
]
 The game's translation and 
Resource Packs
 will 
not
 affect name-based item drops: the Guide can only drop the 
Green Cap
 if he is originally named 
Andrew
. Using a Resource Pack that changes this name does not prevent it from dropping, and changing any other names of the Guide to 
Andrew
 does not qualify for item drop.
While NPCs cannot usually be attacked directly by players, the 
Guide Voodoo Doll
 will allow players to attack the Guide.
This trait is shared with the 
Clothier
 with the 
Clothier Voodoo Doll
.
Killing the Guide in 
The Underworld
 via the use of a Guide Voodoo Doll, or allowing the Guide to die in the Underworld while he is in contact with 
lava
, will summon the 
Wall of Flesh
.
[
3
]
The Guide, like any other friendly NPC, will not respawn unless it is 
daytime
.
The stats of 
weapons
 shown in the Guide's crafting recipe list use adjusted 
damage
 and 
critical strike chance
 based on any bonuses the player currently has equipped, such as the 
Molten armor
's set bonus. Un-equipping items that adjust damage and critical strike chance while speaking to the Guide allows seeing the base values.
Killing the Guide grants the player a minor, temporary 
luck
 boost 
(see 
Luck § Ladybug luck
 for details)
.
The Guide does not provide information about 
Shimmer transmutations
 of items.
The Guide provides hints on how to summon every boss, with the exception of 
Queen Slime
 and the 
Empress of Light
.
The Guide is replaced as the starting NPC by various other NPCs in 
special world seeds
 and 
secret world seeds
:
The 
Demolitionist
 is the starting NPC in 
For the Worthy
 worlds.
The 
Party Girl
 is the starting NPC in 
Drunk
 worlds.
The 
Merchant
 is the starting NPC in 
Not the Bees
 worlds.
The 
Tax Collector
 is the starting NPC in 
Remix
 worlds.
The 
Zoologist
 is the starting NPC in 
Vampirism
 worlds.

Trivia
[
edit
]
One of the Guide's names is Andrew, named after Andrew Spinks (aka 
Redigit
), the lead developer of 
Terraria
.
The Green Cap is dropped if a Guide with that name is killed.
The Guide's Shimmered form is a reference to Redigit's avatar.
[
5
]
For players who use help from other sources rather than the Guide, his role is usually minimal. Otherwise, he lives up to his name, as a primary source of in-game hints and information.
 
The credits cutscene about the Guide.
Redigit said during an 
AMA
 on Reddit that he created the Guide Voodoo Doll so players could get revenge on him for always opening the door.
[
6
]
The Guide opening doors for 
enemies
 at 
night
 is a recurring joke among the fanbase, which is also referenced during the 
credits
.
The Guide is depicted in two 
paintings
, 
Guide Picasso
 and 
The Creation of the Guide
.
 – According to the 
Terraria
 lore pages
, there is an organization called "Order of the Guide", whose purpose is to preserve old legends and knowledge. The Guide is most likely a member of this order.
This is referenced in-game after beating the Wall of Flesh, with any new Guides that spawn in afterward referencing their predecessor suddenly bursting into flames.
The Guide and the 
Arms Dealer
 appear to not get along. This is evidenced by them frequently arguing through 
emotes
, along with the Arms Dealer's quote “
I heard there is a doll that looks very similar to <name of Guide> somewhere in the underworld.  I'd like to put a few rounds in it.
”. Despite this, they do not have any effect on each other's 
happiness
.
The 
Goblin Tinkerer
 and the 
Mechanic
 will similarly flirt through emotes. These are the only four NPCs to have unique interactions when talking to a specific NPC.
In the past, Redigit has said the Guide is the Wall of Flesh,
[
7
]
 the brother of the ninja seen inside 
King Slime
,
[
8
]
 the great-grandson of the 
Nymph
,
[
9
]
 and the son of the 
Moon Lord
.
[
10
]
 These statements were most likely jokes
[
11
]
 and are not considered official lore.
[
12
]
The Guide says that he has much in common with the Zoologist and the Clothier. This may be referring to the fact that they are all cursed with some sort of transformation that is out of their control (if the Guide is indeed the Wall of Flesh).
According to the 
Voodoo Demon's Bestiary entry
, the Guide is said to be the "chosen one whose soul is linked with the world's guardian himself", most likely referring to the Wall of Flesh.
The Guide has the first 
Bestiary
 entry.
 The 
Bestiary
 entry for the Guide: 
"
The Guide always offers useful advice and crafting recipes. His origins and unusual connections to this world remain a mystery.
"
