Since the Ankh Charm removes existing debuffs immediately when equipped, to save up an accessory slot for other accessories, the player can equip the Ankh Charm in another 
loadout
 and switch to it when necessary. Vanity slots will also work.
Trivia
[
edit
]
A real ankh
The 
Ankh
, the 
ancient Egyptian
 
hieroglyphic symbol
 that is depicted on the item, was most commonly used in writing and in art to represent the word for "life" and, by extension, as a symbol of life itself. The Ankh symbol is seen in many depictions of Egyptian deities, where it symbolized eternal life. These deities were depicted as holding the Ankh by the loop at the top.
This symbol also appears on the 
Ruinous Staff
.
This item appears to be an 
omamori
, a Japanese 
Shinto
 charm made of decorative paper, designed to bring good luck and protection to its wearer. Omamori commonly have a pentagonal shape and feature a written prayer in the center, similar to how the Ankh symbol appears in the center of the Ankh Charm. The hole at the top of an omamori would be used to put a string through, so that the charm could be worn like a necklace or hung off of an outfit or on one's home.
