The 
Cell Phone
 is a post-
Skeletron
 
informational item
 and 
tool
 that combines the functions of the 
PDA
 and the 
Magic Mirrors
, displaying all information and allowing the player to teleport to their 
spawn point
. Unlike most informational items, the Cell Phone does not double as an 
accessory
. It instead functions solely from the player's main 
inventory
.

Trivia
[
edit
]
The Cell Phone has one of the most complex crafting trees in 
Terraria
,
 using 14 base items (and 7 crafting operations), beaten by its upgrade, the 
Shellphone
, which uses 16, and beaten again by the 
Zenith
, which uses 22.
The Cell Phone's nature as a single "catch-all" device parallels the multi-functionality of real modern 
smartphones
, which have largely rendered once-common appliances like watches, compasses, timers, radios, and atlases for the most part obsolete. The usage of a mirror in the recipe could likewise be a reference how a smartphone's 
selfie
 camera may be used as a mirror. The mirror could also be because of the mirror-like touchscreen, which is vastly different from the older model phones which used buttons, or the "home" button found on many cell phones, due to the mirror being used to take the player "home."
It does not seem to be based on any particular real-life smartphone models.
The Cell Phone is one of four non-
accessories
 that is crafted at a 
Tinkerer's Workshop
, the others being the 
Ultrabright Helmet
, 
Multicolor Wrench
, and 
The Grand Design
.
Despite its 
rarity
 of 
, the Cell Phone can be acquired 
pre-Hardmode
.
It has one of the slowest 
use times
 in the game, tied with the 
Magic Conch
, 
Demon Conch
, 
Magic Mirrors
, 
Shellphone
, and 
Snake Charmer's Flute
.
